# Limitations and deviations

This branch directly tests the paper's exact construction and exact formulas,
not nearby generic inequalities. It is nevertheless finite. It cannot by itself
verify C1/C2's universal quantifiers or C3 beyond the tested orders, and therefore
its result is reported as finite corroboration rather than `VERIFIED`. A later
child must add an independently reconstructed symbolic proof certificate before
any theorem-level verdict is promoted.

The paper gives no numerical value for the universal `N0` in C3. Orders that do
not yet satisfy the final sharpness inequality are retained rather than hidden.
