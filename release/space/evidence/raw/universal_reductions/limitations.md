# Limitations

This is an exact symbolic proof certificate for the implication chains, not a
kernel-checked formalization of real analysis or probability theory. The
certificate therefore exposes a premise ledger for every imported analytic or
statistical theorem. In particular, it relies on the weighted-polynomial
lemmas proved in the pinned paper, Jia et al.'s separately pinned
local-entropy/Fano result, and Chen--Gao--Ren's separately pinned equal-law
lemma.

The six direct Chebyshev calculations remain finite corroboration only. They
are not used to discharge a universal or asymptotic quantifier.
